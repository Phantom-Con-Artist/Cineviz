# Changelog

## 1.0 (2026)

The first full release.

### The fight
- Choreography on the beat grid: blows on eighth notes once the fight heats up, set pieces timed so their impact lands on the drop.
- Eight fighters with their own styles, stances, supermoves and ultramoves, and a cast tab with their (invented) stories.
- 51 weapon sets built on ten archetypes; weapon weight changes how fighters move. Hybrid combat: weapons thrown, planted, recalled and manifested mid-combo.
- Ten new supermoves and fifteen arena-scale ultramoves, each with anticipation, formation, release, impact and aftermath.
- A speedster vocabulary (dashes, multi-strikes, phantom assaults, velocity breaks) and the Rush flurry.
- Every show rolls a kind of fight: Speed Blitz, Street Brawl, Sword Saints, Sky War, Power Surge or Rampage.
- A combo counter and a VS title card.

### The world
- The arena breaks as the fight goes on: cracks in reality across the sky, a rift of star trails, the moon splitting apart, scarred floors, slabs heaved up by shockwaves and toppling monoliths, all with mass and inertia.
- At the end the winner plants their weapon in the ground and the camera circles the ruins.
- The floor, rocks, motes and sky react to the kick and the bass.

### The camera
- A virtual director with worm's-eye and long-lens scale shots for ultramoves, rationed slow motion and a catch-up speed ramp.
- Watch mode (no UI, full screen) and free cam (fly the camera yourself).

### Everything else
- Adaptive performance: an integrated-graphics profile and automatic resolution and effect scaling.
- Phone layout in landscape, full screen on first touch.
- About panel and a new particle logo.
