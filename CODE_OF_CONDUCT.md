# Code of Conduct

Cineviz is a small project made for fun. Let's keep it a friendly place.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect that people have different backgrounds, skills and opinions.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break these rules, and may block contributors who keep doing so. To report a problem, contact the maintainer, Subhradeep Sarkar, privately through the project's repository. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
